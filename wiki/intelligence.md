# On Intelligence

> "The moment when you look at the falseness of things and see clearly the false as the false, that perception is the beginning of intelligence."

> "If you see something false, to be actually, factually false, and you hold on to that false — you are not intelligent."

> "There is security only in intelligence. Intelligence of that kind is supreme security."

> "The ending of sorrow is the beginning of wisdom and therefore intelligence."

> "The act of compassion is the act of intelligence."
