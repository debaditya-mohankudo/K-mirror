## How to Use

1. **Search by theme** — Use the theme-based wiki files ([Becoming](../becoming.md), [Desire](../desire.md), etc.) for focused inquiry.
2. **Deep dive** — Read a full talk transcript for context and nuance.
3. **Cross-reference** — Check the theme files to see which talks touched on your inquiry question.

## Extracting Quotes

When a new transcript is added, quotes are curated and appended to the corresponding theme files in the `wiki/` directory (e.g., `wiki/becoming.md`, `wiki/desire.md`, etc.).
