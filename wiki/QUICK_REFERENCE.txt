                        TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

→ uv pip install --force-reinstall -e .

anthropic_api_key not set
→ echo "anthropic_api_key=sk-YOUR-KEY" > .env

Database locked
→ pkill -f "python.*k-mirror"
