# Wiki Navigation Guide

Use this guide to find the right theme for a given inquiry. Each path starts with a primary theme, then suggests deepening branches.

---

## If Inquiring About **Suffering & Release**

*For someone caught in pain, loss, or emotional turmoil*

**Primary Path:**
1. [Sorrow](sorrow.md) — understand the nature of suffering as common to all
2. [Attachment](attachment.md) — see how clinging perpetuates pain
3. [Death & Living](death-living.md) — explore ending through dissolution, not escape

**Deepening:**
- [Observation](observation.md) — attention dissolves sorrow
- [Love](love.md) — compassion is the ending of sorrow
- [Intelligence](intelligence.md) — wisdom emerges from understanding pain

---

## If Inquiring About **The Self / Identity Crisis**

*For someone questioning "who am I?" or feeling fragmented*

**Primary Path:**
1. [Consciousness-Identity](consciousness-identity.md) — the illusion of separate self
2. [Memory-Identity](memory-identity.md) — the ego as accumulated memory
3. [Observation](observation.md) — seeing the self clearly dissolves false identity

**Deepening:**
- [Controller & Controlled](controller.md) — no actual division between observer and observed
- [Relationship](relationship.md) — you are not separate from all humanity
- [Death & Living](death-living.md) — dying to identity constructed from the past

---

## If Inquiring About **Fear & False Security**

*For someone anxious, defensive, or clinging to safety*

**Primary Path:**
1. [Fear](fear.md) — fear is born from thought's projections into the future
2. [Security-Aloneness](security-aloneness.md) — true security is not in things or beliefs
3. [Becoming](becoming.md) — seeking security through becoming perpetuates division

**Deepening:**
- [Attachment](attachment.md) — fear drives clinging to the known
- [Thought](thought.md) — understanding time/thought ends fear
- [Observation](observation.md) — clarity brings stability

## If Inquiring About **Love & Relationship**

*For someone struggling with connection, jealousy, or possessiveness*

**Primary Path:**
1. [Love](love.md) — love is not desire, memory, or possession
2. [Relationship](relationship.md) — images and memories prevent real meeting
3. [Attachment](attachment.md) — attachment corrupts relationship

**Deepening:**

## If Inquiring About **Change & Becoming**

*For someone trying to transform, improve, or achieve something*

**Primary Path:**
1. [Becoming](becoming.md) — the desire to become perpetuates duality and conflict
2. [Thought](thought.md) — thought moves away from "what is" into illusion
3. [Observation](observation.md) — observation shows the unreality of becoming

**Deepening:**
- [Death & Living](death-living.md) — true change requires dying to the known *now*
- [Desire](desire.md) — desire to become is endlessly seeking

## Cross-Theme Inquiry

When an inquiry touches multiple areas, follow the connections in the "Related Themes" section of each file. For example:

- **"Why do I fear death?"** → [Fear](fear.md) → [Death & Living](death-living.md) → [Attachment](attachment.md) → [Sorrow](sorrow.md)
- **"How do I love without jealousy?"** → [Love](love.md) → [Relationship](relationship.md) → [Observation](observation.md) → [Attachment](attachment.md)
- **"Can I really change?"** → [Becoming](becoming.md) → [Thought](thought.md) → [Death & Living](death-living.md) → [Observation](observation.md)
