# On Sorrow

> "Suffering is the same whether it is yours, mine or another's. We may interpret that suffering in a different way, we may have a different cause for it, but essentially suffering is common to all man. So it is not your suffering but human suffering."

> "Sorrow is not at the moment of losing somebody — it has begun long ago. All your acts have brought about this isolation, of which you are unconscious, which we take for granted. And sorrow may be the indication of what you have done."

> "Sorrow may be the indication that dependence, attachment is corruption."

> "A mind that lives in sorrow can never be free. It is only a mind that is totally free from sorrow that will know what compassion is."

> "The ending of sorrow is the beginning of wisdom and therefore intelligence."
