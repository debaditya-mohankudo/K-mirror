# On Sensation & Image: Why Sex Has Become So Important

## Core Observation

The question "why has sex become so important now" is almost verbatim one put to Krishnamurti in *The First and Last Freedom*. His answer is the spine of this file, and the ontology formalises it.

> "The act itself can never be a problem, but thought about the act creates the problem… When there is no love in your heart, what remains is pleasure, and therefore sex becomes a tremendous problem." *(The First and Last Freedom)*

Sex is inflated because it is **the one area of life left where thought and the self briefly subside** — a taste of self-forgetting. Everywhere else we are second-hand: our work, beliefs, ambitions and opinions are handed to us, mechanical, imitative. Sex is the last door to ecstasy that is not obviously borrowed, so the whole weight of a thwarted life gets loaded onto it. The importance of sex is a **measure of how conditioned everything else has become**.

---

## The Psychological Pattern

### 1. Why it *feels* important — a counterfeit of the ending

In the sensory moment, before thought re-enters, the centre drops. That momentarily resembles `k:Attention` with no centre and `k:Actuality` (`ofThought: false`) — the nearest thing to freedom most people ever touch. Naturally it gets prized.

### 2. Why it *becomes a problem* — pathway P2 captures it

> `Perception → Contact → Sensation → Thought creates Image → Desire → Will → PursuitOfPleasure → [thwarted] → Fear | Antagonism | Violence`

> "Seeing, touching, sensation — then thought creates the image of living in that house, being with that woman or man… and at that moment desire is born." *(Desire)*

Thought takes the memory of the moment, builds a picture, and pursues its repetition. What was `k:Actuality` is now `k:Construct`. *"Remembered beauty is not beauty."*

### 3. Why pursuit escalates rather than satisfies — A16 + A8

`Construct ∪ Construct = Construct ≠ Actuality`. The image-driven pursuit is an open set enlarging itself — more novelty, more frequency, more scenes — and it never converges on the thought-free moment it is chasing, because (A8) thought cannot manufacture the absence of thought. The *importance* is the size of that gap. The more sex is pursued as image, the more it is needed and the less it delivers.

### 4. Why it turns to conflict — P2's tail, A11

> "Where there is desire, with its image created by thought, there must be the pursuit of pleasure. And when pleasure is thwarted, either there is fear, or antagonism, or violence." *(Desire)*

`Pleasure invariably goes with fear.` `Attachment → Corruption ∧ Fear ∧ Sorrow` (A11). By P4–P5 the relationship is two images in contact — *"the other is a memory"* — so the intimacy is construct meeting construct, which is why it disappoints and why jealousy is structural, not accidental.

### 5. Why it is an escape — A9 / `k:Escape`

Sex as flight from the daily emptiness, boredom, loneliness — from the self. *"We have made it into an extraordinary thing… because everything else has become mechanical."*

## Why *Now* Specifically

The contemporary intensifiers each amplify a particular node in the graph:

- **Sex as identity and self-worth.** Modern culture treats sexuality as "the primary way to achieve personal worth, identity, and fulfilment," inside a therapeutic culture that "idolises self-realisation." In ontology terms: `k:Becoming` and `k:Identity` fused onto sex — a project to *be someone* — and `k:FalseSecurity` in the A17 sense, "I am this sexual self" demanded as a fixed point.
- **Industrial image-production.** Pornography and a sexualised media feed deliver `thought creates Image` (P2's third step) outsourced to screens, pre-made and endless, so `k:Desire` is manufactured upstream of any actual `Sensation`.
- **The comparison engine.** Feeds rank bodies and encounters; the "oversharing era" makes sexual life a thing to display and measure. That is A10 — `Comparison → Becoming → Duality → Conflict` — applied to sex at scale.
- **The other doors have closed.** Sex swells to fill the space left by a mechanical life. The traditional counterweights to self-forgetting — shared religion, craft, unhurried contact with nature, community — have thinned, while the second-hand quality of work and opinion has deepened. Fewer `ofThought: false` openings remain, so the whole load lands on the one that is left.

## The Ontology's Own Exit (stated, not prescribed)

Not suppression, not indulgence, not transcendence — *"to understand it, to look at it, to see the whole momentum of it"*: `k:SeeingTheFalse` of P2 as a whole. The act is not the problem; the image is. `Image obscures Love`. Drop the image-making and what is left is not a lesser sex life but the thing that was never in the graph to begin with.

By A13 this map is itself `ofThought` — check it against your own observation, then drop it.

## Inquiry Questions

- When sex feels important, what exactly is being sought — the sensation, or the image thought builds around it?
- Can you see the moment thought steps in after the sensation and starts making a picture?
- Is the pursuit ever finished? Or does each satisfaction rebuild the wanting?
- What are you afraid would be there if this door were not available?
- Everywhere else in your life you are second-hand. Is that the real weight sex is being asked to carry?
- Can the whole movement — sensation, image, desire, pursuit, thwarting — be watched without the watcher who wants to fix it?
