# On the Controller & Controlled

> "Thought has made the thinker separate from thought, which then says, 'I must control'."

> "The thinker is the thought. The controller is the controlled."

> "When the controller is the controlled, then you remove totally conflict. Conflict exists only when there is division."

> "Our life is conflict because we live with this division. But this division is fallacious, is not real — it has become our habit, our culture, to control."

> "When there is control, there are two entities: the one who is controlling, controlling that which he himself has created. I control my desire — which means the one controlling is a division from the thing called desire. Therefore there is conflict where there is division."

> "To live without any sense of conflict — therefore no sense of control. Then the mind is absolutely still."

> "The observer is the observed. The thinker is the thought. There is no division. And therefore there's no time psychologically between them." *(from Krishnamurti & David Bohm: The Future of Humanity part 1 of 2)*

> "The attributes, the qualities, the virtues—all the judgments and conclusions—they constitute the me. So I am my attributes, my thought creates the sense of me." *(from Krishnamurti & David Bohm: The Future of Humanity part 1 of 2)*

> "If I say I am greedy, then greed is not different from me. Greeding is me. But the ordinary way of thinking is that I am here, and greed is an attribute I may or may not have." *(from Krishnamurti & David Bohm: The Future of Humanity part 1 of 2)*

> "Anything that is limited must inevitably create conflict. The me is limited, therefore the me creates conflict." *(from Krishnamurti & David Bohm: Brockwood Park 1983, Conversation 1)*
