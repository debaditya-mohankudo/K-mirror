# On the Controller & Controlled

> "Thought has made the thinker separate from thought, which then says, 'I must control'."

> "The thinker is the thought. The controller is the controlled."

> "When the controller is the controlled, then you remove totally conflict. Conflict exists only when there is division."

> "Our life is conflict because we live with this division. But this division is fallacious, is not real — it has become our habit, our culture, to control."

> "When there is control, there are two entities: the one who is controlling, controlling that which he himself has created. I control my desire — which means the one controlling is a division from the thing called desire. Therefore there is conflict where there is division."

> "To live without any sense of conflict — therefore no sense of control. Then the mind is absolutely still."
