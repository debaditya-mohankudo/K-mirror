# On Seeing the False

> "To see the false as the false — which means you have already discovered what is true."

> "When you see something false, when you see the limited activities of thought and what it does in the world — the very seeing of the fact is the ending of that illusion. Not that you will conclude to end it; the very seeing of the fact is the ending."

> "When my friend sees that the conditioning of the brain has broken — I have been going north all my life and you point out that north is an illusion. We talk it over and I say, by Jove, that's right, and I turn. Which means I have broken the habit, the condition of the brain. Therefore the cells themselves have changed."

> "Doubt, the seed of doubt — not what you doubt, but the seed. Let it move, let it flower, let it grow till it finds what is truth."
