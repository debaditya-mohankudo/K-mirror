# On Attachment

> "Where there is attachment I recognise, observe there is corruption."

> "We are attached because we are lonely — deep abiding loneliness which is suffocating, isolating, depressing."

> "When I am attached to a belief, hoping in that attachment there will be certain security — that attachment not only prevents further examination, but I am frightened to examine even when I am greatly attached to something."

> "The other is a memory: my wife, my husband, my children, are physically different from me — psychologically the memory of my wife, I am attached to that, to the name, to the form."
